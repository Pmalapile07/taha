Who we are

TAHA Cleaning & Custodial Solutions (TAHA CCS) is a rising cleaning and custodial company providing quality, affordable, tailor-made cleaning for residential and commercial clients. Our experienced team delivers high-quality cleaning, disinfection, and maintenance built around the unique needs of each space.

Our mission is to provide quality cleaning that exceeds expectations on effectiveness, cost, environment, and customer satisfaction. Our values are integrity, responsibility, innovation, discipline, and team work.
